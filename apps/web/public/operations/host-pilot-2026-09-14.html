Host conversation and session checklist

  1. Ask what makes a first-time participant difficult to welcome, how cancellations are handled, and whether the host wants activity-only, friendship or an explicitly dating-oriented session. Explain that existing community members are never silently enrolled in a dating pilot.
  2. Ask whether an adult organizer can commit to one specific date and a backup/cancellation plan. A positive reply is a conversation, not a host commitment. Count a commitment only after an adult host agrees to a format, date, capacity and responsibility scope. Keep personal contacts outside public reports.
  3. Confirm a suitable public venue, equipment, beginner expectations, accessibility information, weather fallback, first-aid/emergency responsibilities, local permissions, and any charges. No claim of medical supervision or universal accessibility. Anything requiring money, terms, insurance representations or legal commitments returns to the owner before agreement.
  4. Before a public event exists, verify the deployed product's adult, join/accept, capacity, cancellation, block/report and support paths. Confirm a real person can handle safety reports during the pilot. A test failure or absent operator blocks the session, while preparation continues.
  5. Create a proposed 45-minute format: 5-minute welcome and intention explanation, 25-minute gentle activity, 10-minute optional conversation, 5-minute close. Set a maximum of 4–6 participants only if the host and activity permit it; padel capacity depends on an actual approved court arrangement.
  6. Publish only after owner-authorized launch scope and host agreement. Public invitation shares the broad area and activity; precise participant meeting details remain behind accepted membership. Do not publish host private contact details, attendance lists, orientations or private messages.
  7. Host reviews requests, accepts within capacity, and communicates changes through the product. Cancellation is acceptable; no shame, forced pairing, attendance penalties or pressure to share phone numbers. Everyone may leave. No photos without specific permission.
  8. Afterward, collect aggregate attendance and optional private feedback: Was the purpose clear? Was arriving comfortable? Would you attend again? Route incidents through the actual support process. Never infer “safe” merely because no report was submitted.
